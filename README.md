# Imperium

**AI driven property management for landlords, on iPad**

From the developer of crassus.pro comes the AI powered future of real property management from the pocket: Crassus' Imperium. Like crassus.pro - Imperium keeps your properties, tenants, leases, invoices, expenses and VAT in one place, but expands by leveraging the latest Apple software tools. Use Siri to inquire into the status of your Empire and have it mark open invoices as billed, or send chasers. 

Imperium is Build for iPad to maximize functionality whilst keeping visibility. An iPhone Duo version is under consideration.

## About the app

### Properties and tenants
- Keep all your properties on a map, with the outline and area of your portfolio.
- Track each lessee, lease, lease term and renewal.
- See the rent history of each lease: indexations, renewals and manual changes, shown as a chart.
- Get reminders before notice deadlines and when an indexation is overdue. Imperium can also email the lessee to confirm an indexation.

### Invoicing
- Create invoices one by one, or prepare a whole month in one batch. 
- Drafts stay editable. An issued invoice is final and gets a sequential number with no gaps; corrections go through credit notes.
- Rent is prorated by the day when it starts, ends or changes mid-month.
- Invoices are sent as PDFs through the Mail app, in Dutch, English, German, French or Italian.
- Record payments and send reminders. The dashboard shows what's billed, collected and overdue.

### Expenses
- Add expenses by scanning a paper receipt, importing a file, choosing a photo or typing them in.
- Imperium reads the supplier, date, amounts, VAT, IBAN and VAT ID, and checks them before you confirm.
- Duplicates are recognised and refused.

### VAT report
- Get a VAT summary per month, quarter or year: VAT charged, VAT incurred, and what's not included and why.
- Export a CSV, plus a package with all invoice PDFs and expense originals for your accountant, or email it straight to the beancounter.

### Siri
- Ask Siri for a status overview, or for your open invoices. From that list you can mark an invoice paid or send a reminder.

**Requirements:** an iPad with iPadOS 27 or later. Sending invoices by email needs an account set up in the Mail app. Apple Intelligence is optional; without it, Imperium falls back to its own pattern matching when reading expenses.

## Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 28 September 2026*

**The short version: Imperium does not collect any data. Everything you enter stays on your iPad.**

### What Imperium stores
Imperium stores the information you enter on your device only. That covers properties, lessees, leases, invoices, expenses, scanned documents and settings. It has no user accounts, no servers and no iCloud sync. The developer cannot see, access or receive any of it.

### What Imperium does not do
- No analytics, tracking or advertising.
- No third-party SDKs that collect data.
- No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

### Apple services the app uses
A few features rely on Apple's system services, which fall under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/):
- **Maps and address lookup:** to place a property on the map, Imperium sends the address you entered to Apple Maps to look up its coordinates. Map images are loaded from Apple Maps. Imperium never asks for your own location.
- **Apple Intelligence** (optional): reading expense documents and rewording reminders happens on your device, using Apple's on-device models.
- **Siri:** the Siri features work through Apple's App Intents system.

### Camera, photos and files
- **Camera:** used only when you choose to scan a receipt or invoice.
- **Photos and files:** Imperium only sees the photo or file you pick. It has no access to the rest of your library or files.

### Email and sharing
Invoices, reminders, indexation letters and VAT reports are sent only when you choose to, through the Mail app and your own email account, or through the share sheet. Once sent, they're handled by your email provider and the recipient.

### Backups
Your data is part of your iPad's device backup (iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer) if you have that turned on. Those backups are managed by you and Apple.

### Deleting your data
Delete records inside the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your iPad. Existing device backups keep a copy until they're replaced or deleted.

### Children
Imperium is a business app and is not directed at children.

### Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

## Support

### Getting help
Email support@crassus.pro with a short description of the issue and your iPadOS version. Please don't send sensitive tenant data unless it's needed to reproduce the problem.

### Frequently asked questions

**Can I use Imperium on more than one device?**
No. Imperium is iPad-only and keeps its data on one device, so invoice numbers are always unique and free of gaps.

**How do I back up my data?**
Your data is included in your iPad's regular backup (iCloud Backup or Finder). Restoring that backup to a new iPad brings your data with it.

**Sending an invoice says Mail isn't set up.**
Imperium sends through the Mail app. Add an email account under Settings → Apps → Mail → Mail Accounts. You can also export and share the PDF another way using the share sheet.

**Can I change or delete an issued invoice?**
No. An issued invoice is final and keeps its number. Issue a credit note for the full or partial amount, then create a new invoice if needed.

**Why can't I delete a property, lessee or lease?**
Records that invoices refer to are protected, so your history stays complete. A lease that has never been invoiced can be deleted.

**Expense fields were read incorrectly.**
Every scanned expense stays in *Needs Review* until you check and confirm it, and you can correct any field before confirming. Results are better on devices with Apple Intelligence turned on.

**Does Imperium file my VAT return?**
No. It prepares the figures and documents for you or your accountant. Whether your VAT is deductible, including pro rata cases, is up to you to decide.
